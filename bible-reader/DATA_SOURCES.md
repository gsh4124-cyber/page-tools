# Initial Korean reading content

The default Korean reader contains the unmodified KRV John 3 verses in its HTML
and a JSON seed for all 21 chapters of John. The reader uses the same data format
as its existing remote KRV source. Other books and translations retain their
existing sources. The shared foreign-language loader removes this Korean seed
and static chapter before initializing the localized reader.

The Korean Bible Society states that the KRV economic-rights protection period
has expired and requires attribution and preservation of the work:
https://www.bskorea.or.kr/bbs/board.php?bo_table=copyright_faq&wr_id=5

The seed is a snapshot of the existing third-party dataset, not a claim of an
official, error-free Bible Society digital edition. Compare the source revision
before updating it; do not silently rewrite the Scripture text.
